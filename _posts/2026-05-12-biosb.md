Members of AIT4Life group attended [BioSB 2026](https://biosb2026.aanmelder.nl/), the 12th edition of the annual Dutch conference on bioinformatics and systems biology. The conference took place May 11–13 at De Berckt in Baarlo. BioSB brings together researchers, educators, and industry partners from across the Netherlands to share advances and foster collaboration in computational life sciences. That was a lot of fun!
