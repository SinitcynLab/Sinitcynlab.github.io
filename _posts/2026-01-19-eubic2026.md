Grigory and Liza have joined the [EuBIC-MS Winter School 2026](https://eubic-ms.org/events/2026-winter-school/) on computational mass spectrometry–based omics in Harrachov, Czech Republic.

The winter school brings together early-career researchers and experts for keynote lectures, hands-on workshops, and networking, covering topics like computational proteomics/metabolomics, single-cell proteomics, immunopeptidomics, and multi-omics integration, with a strong focus on advanced workflows and AI-driven bioinformatics.
